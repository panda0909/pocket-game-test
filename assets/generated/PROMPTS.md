# 美術生成 Prompt 組

目前執行中的素材使用 `stock_*` 命名；舊版同名素材保留在本資料夾作為歷史參考。

## 共用風格

Premium 2D mobile tower-defense game art, deep navy and teal battlefield,
warm gold paths, cyan friendly energy, coral-red danger accents, thick dark
violet outlines, chunky readable silhouettes, cute sci-fi adventure tone,
high contrast, polished hand-painted finish, designed for a 720×1280 portrait
mobile game. No Chinese or English text, no numbers, no watermark.
